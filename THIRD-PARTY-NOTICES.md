## iLEAPP and aLEAPP

suiteDFIR runs the official iLEAPP and aLEAPP release builds. They are not bundled: the app downloads them (or imports them offline) only when the user asks it to, verifies their SHA-256, and runs them unmodified.

- iLEAPP: <https://github.com/abrignoni/iLEAPP>
- aLEAPP: <https://github.com/abrignoni/ALEAPP>
